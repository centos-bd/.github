# Code of Conduct — CentOS Kernel Community Bangladesh

> Based on Contributor Covenant 3.0 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/), the current industry standard. We adopt its principles and adapt enforcement to our community. Please read the canonical text for the full definition.

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make our community welcoming, safe, and equitable for everyone — regardless of background, identity, or experience level. Participation is a privilege extended to all who act in good faith.

## Encouraged Behaviours
- Be kind, honest, and patient — especially with beginners.
- Respect different viewpoints, cultures, and levels of English/Bangla fluency.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility when you make a mistake, and help repair harm.
- Focus on the technical idea, not the person.

## Restricted Behaviours
We do not tolerate:
- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks in any form.
- Discriminatory language or jokes targeting identity or background.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or events.
- Misrepresenting yourself or failing to credit others' work.

## Scope
This applies to all community spaces: Telegram/Discord, GitHub, mailing lists, meetups, workshops, and any event representing the community.

## Reporting
If you experience or witness a violation:
- Email: [conduct@centos-bd.org — REPLACE with your real address before launch]
- You may also DM any organiser directly.

Reports will be acknowledged within 72 hours. We will respect your privacy and never require you to confront the person directly.

## Enforcement
Organisers will follow a restorative, escalating approach:
1. **Correction** — private reminder and request to adjust behaviour.
2. **Warning** — formal warning with clear expectations.
3. **Temporary suspension** — removal from some or all spaces for a defined period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for serious or repeated violations.

Organisers who are the subject of a report will recuse themselves from the decision. We aim to repair harm and, where possible, reintegrate members who show genuine accountability.
